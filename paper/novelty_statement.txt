We evaluate how a multi-camera trajectory-length measurement should divide a
fixed processing budget between simultaneous views and sampling instants.
At 84 frames per window, a calibration-selected three-view configuration
achieves lower observed error than seven views on the same confirmation
windows. Explicit record-level comparisons, completion reporting and
reference-sensitivity analysis qualify the resulting design guidance.
Overlapping-subset simulations, disjoint-camera comparisons and a robust
triangulation control test possible explanations without assuming a universal
saturation mechanism. The study provides a reproducible approach to evaluating
frame-budget allocation for a specified measurement chain.

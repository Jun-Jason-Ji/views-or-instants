We evaluate camera-view and sampling-time allocation under a frame-processing budget, jointly with sampling-grid sensitivity and outlier handling. Across eleven schedules at two budgets, fixed spatial checks remove an apparent adverse three-view ranking without losing completed windows; additional temporal gating does not consistently help. A retrospective development-only procedure makes completion and tail-error constraints explicit. The contribution is a reproducible evaluation of interacting measurement choices, with preserved negative results and clearly stated limits on independent validation and transfer.
